# WikiText-2 dataset

**Cluster:** [[Phase 1 - Decoder-only GPT]]

**Intuition.** A clean Wikipedia LM benchmark with real casing/punctuation (~2.09M training
tokens, 600 articles); a good first decoder-only target. Phases 1 and 2 use it for a direct
A/B comparison at equal parameter count.

## Reference
- "Pointer Sentinel Mixture Models," Merity, Xiong, Bradbury & Socher, 2016 - arXiv:1609.07843.
